Core rationale

The 8WTC flywheel

A funnel ends at the purchase. A flywheel feeds its output back in: transformations become creative, app data becomes audiences, members bring members. Media adds speed; removing friction keeps it turning.

Force — what speeds it upSpend behind proven UGC, app-event audiences, referrals.
Friction — what we removeSlow LPs, broken deep links, onboarding drop-off, untracked installs.

Back to Attract. New UGC lowers CPM and lifts CTR; finishers seed lookalikes; referrals add free installs. That is why CPA should fall cohort over cohort, not rise.

Why this split

  • Meta leads: Reels UGC is both the attract engine and where stage-4 stories re-enter the wheel.
  • App search buys the cheapest high-intent installs; Google grows with Android-heavy markets.
  • Programmatic moves from re-engagement (A) to prospecting (C) as the installer pool grows.

Gates between plans

  • A → B after 4 weeks with CPA under £60 and install → entry at 5%+.
  • B → C when US CPA is under £75 and UGC delivers 20+ new assets a month.
  • Step back if any geo runs two weeks above £109 CPA.
